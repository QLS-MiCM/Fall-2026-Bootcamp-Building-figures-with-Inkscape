## Notes

Participants are encouraged to bring PDF figures generated in R or Python, either from previous workshops or their own projects. For those who do not have their own figures, a collection of mock RNA-seq analysis outputs will be provided to practice constructing a publication-quality multi-panel figure. Mock figures were generated using RNA-seq counts from this [study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2021.04.028).
